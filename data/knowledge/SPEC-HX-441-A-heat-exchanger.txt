Inspection notes
External CUI (corrosion under insulation) is the dominant damage mechanism at the saddle and at insulation penetrations.
Orange-brown product on jacketing seams is a CUI indicator. Strip a 300 mm band and UT if staining is active.
Do not restart steam to the bundle if a flange weepage is visible until bolting integrity is confirmed.
