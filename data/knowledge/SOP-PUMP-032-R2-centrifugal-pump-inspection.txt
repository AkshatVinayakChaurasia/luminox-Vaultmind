2.1 Seal and bearing housing
Look for oil wetness at the bearing housing drain and at the mechanical seal gland.
A light mist film after a long run may be acceptable if the level in the constant-level oiler is stable.
Active dripping, pooling, or dark varnish streaks require a work order and vibration check.

--- PAGE 5 ---
3.4 Coupling guard and base
Loose or missing coupling-guard fasteners are a safety hold.
Foundation grout cracks wider than 2 mm near the pump feet should be logged and compared with the last alignment report.
Paint blistering on the volute often tracks through-wall corrosion on older CS casings — treat as Grade C until UT confirms otherwise.

--- PAGE 7 ---
4.2 Vibration and temperature (field)
Normal bearing housing temperature: less than 75 °C.
Investigate if temperature exceeds 85 °C or if vibration is reported as “rough” by operations.
Do not rely on a photograph alone for vibration; use the photo to locate leaks, rust, and missing hardware.
