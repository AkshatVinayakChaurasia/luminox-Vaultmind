3.2 Visual classification of external corrosion
Grade A — Light surface rust, mill scale remaining, no measurable metal loss.
Grade B — General rust with scattered pitting. Remaining wall typically within 90% of nominal.
Grade C — Widespread pitting or scab corrosion. Remaining wall between 80% and 90% of nominal. Requires thickness survey within 30 days.
Grade D — Deep pitting, flaking scale, or leak stain. Remaining wall may be below retirement thickness. Isolate and notify Inspection immediately.

--- PAGE 12 ---
5.1 Acceptance criteria for carbon steel process piping
Nominal wall for 6-inch Sch 40 CS: 7.11 mm.
Retirement (minimum) thickness: 4.80 mm.
Corrosion allowance: 1.50 mm.
If observed or inferred remaining thickness is below 4.80 mm, the line is unfit for continued service until Fitness-for-Service (API 579) is completed.
If remaining thickness is between 4.80 mm and 6.00 mm, increase inspection frequency to quarterly UT and apply temporary wrap only with Inspection approval.

--- PAGE 14 ---
6.3 Leak stains and product residue
Brown-orange staining below flanges or on insulation jacketing is treated as a possible weepage until proven otherwise.
Photograph, tag the location, and compare against the last inspection report before declaring the finding closed.
Do not disturb heavy scale with impact tools on live hydrocarbon service.

--- PAGE 16 ---
7.1 Recommended actions
Grade A: record and continue routine round.
Grade B: schedule NDT within 90 days.
Grade C: NDT within 30 days; review remaining life.
Grade D: operational restriction and Inspection hold.
